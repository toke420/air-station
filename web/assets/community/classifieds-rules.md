Welcome to the AirStation Classifieds: buy, sell and swap with the community.

**Posting**
- One item (or one bundle) per topic. Put the price and your city or region in the title, e.g. *"Technics 1210 MK2 pair, $1,400, Brisbane"*.
- Add clear photos, the condition and how you'll deliver (pick-up or postage).
- Mark the topic **[SOLD]** when it's gone, so people stop asking.

**Not allowed**
- Counterfeit, bootleg or stolen goods, and anything illegal to sell where you live.
- Spam, affiliate links or reposting the same item more than once a week.

**Stay safe**
- Meet in public places for local deals and test gear before you pay.
- For posted items, use payment methods with buyer protection.
- AirStation doesn't take part in sales and can't refund anyone. Buyers and sellers deal at their own risk.

Moderators may remove listings that break these rules. Questions? Ask in General Discussion.
