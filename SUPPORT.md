# Support

- **E-mail:** [SUPPORT E-MAIL]
- **Licence questions** (lost key, moving to a new server, a server that died without
  being deactivated): include your licence number (L0000…) or purchase e-mail. See
  [docs/LICENSING.md](docs/LICENSING.md).
- **Problems with the station:** include the output of `bash scripts/airctl status` and,
  for the area concerned, `check-config`, `live-check`, `art-doctor` or
  `python3 scripts/community.py status`. Remove passwords, keys and anything private
  first.
